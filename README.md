# EYES TO EARTH | ICYMI
### Seeing the Incomáti from Space

**Digital Editorial Feature | EYES TO EARTH Student Workshop | 31 October**

---

## 1. About the Project

**EYES TO EARTH | ICYMI** is a digital editorial feature documenting the first EYES TO EARTH Student Workshop, held on 31 October, under the theme *"Seeing the Incomáti from Space"*.

The project explores how Earth Observation, satellite technology and remote sensing can contribute to understanding environmental changes and monitoring water resources.

Using the Incomáti River region in Marracuene, Mozambique, as a case study, the workshop connected scientific concepts with practical applications of satellite data analysis.

## 2. About the Workshop

The workshop introduced students to Earth Observation and the applications of satellite technology in environmental monitoring.

Participants explored:

- Landsat and Sentinel satellite missions;
- Optical and radar sensors;
- Spatial resolution and revisit frequency;
- Spectral bands and satellite imagery;
- Remote sensing and environmental applications.

## 3. From Theory to Practice

The practical activities focused on satellite imagery of the Incomáti River region in Marracuene.

Using Google Colab, Jupyter Notebook and Python, participants worked on:

- Satellite image acquisition and visualisation;
- Sentinel and Landsat image comparison;
- Dry and wet season analysis;
- Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI);
- Modified Normalized Difference Water Index (MNDWI);
- Water-detection thresholds;
- Histograms and spectral signatures;
- Water mapping and spatial comparison.

## 7. Technologies and Tools

The project combines web development and scientific computing tools:

- **HTML5** – Website structure.
- **CSS3** – Visual design and responsive layout.
- **Python** – Scientific data processing and analysis.
- **Google Colab** – Cloud-based computational environment.

- **NDWI and MNDWI** – Water detection and monitoring indices.

## 8. Website and Deployment

The project is designed as a responsive digital editorial feature, publicly accessible through GitHub Pages.

To deploy the website:

1. Open the GitHub repository.
2. Navigate to **Settings**.
3. Select **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Select the `main` branch.
6. Choose the `/ (root)` folder.
7. Click **Save**.

Once deployment is complete, GitHub Pages will provide a public URL for the website.
